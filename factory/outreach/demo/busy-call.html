Why busy signals cost $1,500+/mo

During peak dinner rushes (Friday & Saturday 5 to 7:30 PM), phone lines get tied up. Hungry diners don't leave voicemails—they hang up and call another restaurant or order via third-party apps that take 25%–30%.

8–15 missed calls/weekend × $42 avg ticket = $336–$630/weekend ($1,340–$2,500/month) in lost orders.
