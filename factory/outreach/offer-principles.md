# Standing offer principles

User preference recorded October 3, 2026. Apply to Cortese and carry into future business-outreach instructions.

- Design offers around measurable customer benefit and sustainable value for both parties.
- Make the economic case clear using verified facts and explicit assumptions. No guaranteed ROI, fabricated urgency or suggestion that a business is foolish for declining.
- Reduce adoption risk through a focused pilot, clear scope, reasonable usage limits and an easy stop process.
- Keep fees and ongoing obligations clear before activation. A discount must specify what it applies to and for how long.
- Do not quote initial dollar pricing in the Cortese introduction unless the user changes this preference or the recipient asks.
- Stephen must review the final email, demo link and offer before anything is sent. No email has been authorized to send.
- The proposed 30-day pilot and 15% discount require scoped terms before becoming a commitment.

This versioned file preserves the preference. It does not modify the assistant's platform-level instructions or automatically install rules in other CLIs.
