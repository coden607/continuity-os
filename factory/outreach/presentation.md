The slide deck is a private Claude artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/MkqwLiJ99E4SKmxubvM6h7
(Stephen shares it from its Share menu). This file is the text outline of the same deck.

1. **Cover.** A missed call shouldn't mean a missed order.
2. **The problem.** At the rush the line is busy and callers give up. No numbers yet; they
   would come from the carrier's call logs.
3. **Missed-call flow (simulation in the demo).** Busy → forwarded → press 1 → text with the menu link.
   Screens come from the demo's tap-through simulation.
3b. **Customer experience (demo).** Guest screen: "View Cortese Menu" goes to the official menu,
   and "Call to place your order" dials 607-723-6477.
4. **How missed-call recovery would work (planned, not connected).** Busy → press 1 → one
   text with the menu link → the guest calls to order. Callers who don't press 1 hear a voice message.
   The phone-service code has automated tests; live SMS delivery and busy-call forwarding still
   need verification.
5. **Staff workflow (simulated).** Fictional table request → staff inbox → mark reviewing →
   confirm or decline. Stays in the browser; no guest is contacted.
6. **Options.** 1 Missed-call recovery · 2 Table-request inbox · 3 Online ordering (later).
