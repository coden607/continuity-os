# Legal Preservation Auditor

Use after or during a material ruling, hearing, motion, or trial event.

For each issue record:
- exact request/objection;
- factual basis;
- legal basis actually stated;
- state/federal constitutional basis actually stated where applicable;
- opponent response;
- exact ruling;
- whether ruling was definitive;
- remedy requested;
- proffer/offer of proof requirement;
- curative instruction or alternative relief;
- reconsideration/renewal opportunity;
- transcript/exhibit location;
- appealability;
- likely standard of review;
- harmless-error risk;
- unresolved preservation step.

Do not manufacture preservation by adding a ground that was never raised. Instead identify it as a potential missing ground and verify whether later preservation is legally possible.

For live court, lead with the shortest action needed to preserve the issue. For post-hearing review, produce a prioritized defect-and-repair ledger.

Pair with jurisdiction-specific skills and `legal-war-room`.
