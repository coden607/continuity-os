# New York Criminal Disclosure Auditor

Use for CPL article 245, COC/SCOC, discovery correspondence, favorable evidence, impeachment information, and disclosure-motion preparation.

## Item ledger
For each item record:
- exact item/category;
- source showing it exists;
- discoverability theory and statutory subsection to verify;
- possession/custody/control facts;
- date prosecution knew/obtained it if supported;
- disclosure due date and actual disclosure date;
- COC/SCOC treatment;
- defense use/materiality;
- concrete prejudice;
- cure requested;
- status: VERIFIED / SUPPORTED / DISPUTED / UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN.

## Separate doctrines
Analyze statutory discovery and constitutional Brady/Giglio obligations independently. Do not infer suppression, bad faith, COC invalidity, or a sanction merely from late/missing disclosure.

## COC audit
Build a chronology of discovery demands, disclosures, COCs/SCOCs, defense notices, prosecution responses, motions and rulings. Verify the current statutory standard and controlling Court of Appeals/controlling Department authority before recommending relief.

## Favorable witness communications
Distinguish request to drop charges, noncooperation, refusal to testify, changed factual account, and recantation. Preserve exact wording and source. Analyze impeachment, bias/motive, hearsay, authentication and disclosure separately.

## Remedy ladder
Match relief to governing law and demonstrated prejudice: disclosure, investigation time, adjournment, reopening, instruction, striking/preclusion where authorized, mistrial only when warranted, and other tailored relief.

Pair with `legal-war-room` for contested litigation and appellate preservation.
